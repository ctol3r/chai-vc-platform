# Triage SOP

## Issue Triage

### Initial Response SLAs
- **Critical**: 15 minutes
- **High**: 4 hours
- **Medium**: 24 hours
- **Low**: 72 hours

### Triage Owners
- **Security issues**: @security-team
- **Compliance issues**: @legal-compliance
- **Backend bugs**: @backend-team
- **Frontend/UX**: @product
- **Agent issues**: @backend-team + @legal-compliance

## PR Triage

### Review SLAs
- **Hotfix PRs**: 30 minutes
- **Security PRs**: 4 hours
- **Feature PRs**: 48 hours
- **Documentation PRs**: 24 hours

### Escalation Path
1. **Level 1**: Assign to appropriate team
2. **Level 2**: Tag team lead if SLA missed
3. **Level 3**: Engineering manager for critical issues
4. **Level 4**: CTO for security/compliance escalations

## Triage Process
1. **Label** with appropriate tags
2. **Assign** to owner team
3. **Priority** assessment
4. **Timeline** estimation
5. **Track** progress to SLA
