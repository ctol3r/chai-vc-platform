## The Healthcare Credentialing Crisis

Healthcare credential verification is broken. Every year, hospitals spend **$2.1 billion** and **180+ days** per physician on manual credentialing processes that delay patient care, increase administrative burden, and create systemic vulnerabilities in patient safety.

**Current State Problems:**
- 📋 **180-day average** credentialing timeline blocks urgent care access
- 💰 **$7,500-15,000** cost per physician credentialing cycle
- 🏥 **68%** of hospitals report credentialing as top operational challenge
- ⚠️ **Manual verification errors** create patient safety risks
- 🔒 **Privacy concerns** with sensitive medical professional data
- 🌐 **Cross-state verification** adds weeks to locum tenens placements
