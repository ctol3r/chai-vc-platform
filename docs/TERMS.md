# Terms Glossary - Internal Vocabulary

## Core Platform Terms

**Chai VC Platform** - Healthcare credentialing verification platform using zero-knowledge proofs and blockchain technology.

**VITA Token** - Platform utility token used for governance, transaction fees, and network incentives.

**Verifiable Credential (VC)** - Digital credential that can be cryptographically verified without revealing underlying personal information.

**Zero-Knowledge Proof (ZKP)** - Cryptographic method allowing verification of information without revealing the information itself.

**Human-in-the-Loop (HITL)** - Manual review process where human experts review credentials that require additional validation.

**PHI (Protected Health Information)** - Health information covered by HIPAA privacy regulations.

**Primary Source Verification** - Direct verification of credentials with the original issuing authority.

**Joint Commission** - Healthcare accreditation organization that sets credentialing standards.

**NPI (National Provider Identifier)** - Unique identifier for healthcare providers in the US.

## Governance & Economics
